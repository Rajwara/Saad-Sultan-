| File | Used for |

| `avatar-1.jpg` … `avatar-5.jpg` | Testimonial avatars (square, optional): 1 Angelo, 2 Zahran Haji, 3 Sage, 4 Marcos, 5 Muzzam. Their initial shows until you add one. |
| `avatar-me.jpg` | Your small avatar next to "Have something in mind?" |
